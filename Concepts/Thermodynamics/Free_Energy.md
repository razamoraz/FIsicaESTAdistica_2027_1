# Free Energy

## One‑liner
Thermodynamic potential that measures the useful work obtainable from a closed system at constant temperature and volume.

## Core idea
- Defined as $F = U - TS$, bridging microscopic statistical mechanics with macroscopic thermodynamics.
- Directly related to the logarithm of the canonical partition function $Z$.
- Minimization of $F$ defines thermal equilibrium for systems coupled to a heat bath at fixed $(T, V, N)$.

## Key equations
- $$ F = -k_B T \ln Z $$
  - Fundamental relation linking canonical partition function $Z$ to Helmholtz free energy $F$.
- $$ F = U - TS $$
  - Thermodynamic definition of Helmholtz free energy.
